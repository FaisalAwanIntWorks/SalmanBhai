FAQs

Questions parents ask us

What services does your autism center offer?

We provide ABA behaviour therapy, speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, sensory integration, special education and parent counselling — coordinated by one team under one roof.

Does my child need a diagnosis before starting?

No. Many families come to us with concerns only. We start with a detailed assessment, and if needed we guide you on a formal diagnosis while beginning supportive therapy.

What age should therapy start?

We work with children from 18 months onwards. Research shows early intervention leads to the strongest outcomes, but children and teens of every age can make meaningful progress.

How is my child's treatment plan created?

After the assessment, our specialists set measurable goals across communication, behaviour, motor and learning skills. You receive a written plan and we review progress with you every month.

Are parents involved in therapy?

Absolutely. Parents attend regular review meetings and receive home-training so skills carry over into everyday life.

How do I book a consultation?

Fill in the form above, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. The first consultation is free.
